## Suggested tasks from the supplied IA

- **Task A:** You need ten more minutes to finish an assignment. Handle the study-break reminder, then continue studying. Observe discoverability and understanding of delay versus reminder pause.
- **Task B:** Your planned exercise no longer fits. Choose a response and find the updated arrangement. Observe backup/reschedule/skip understanding and whether planning is mistaken for activity completion.
- **Task C:** Review recent progress, correct an inaccurate activity duration, and decide whether to keep the pet. Observe classification, correction feedback, and whether pet-off preserves ordinary progress.

Do not coach button locations before observing. Distinguish independent completion, completion with assistance, and noncompletion. Ask about understanding and pressure without inferring health effectiveness.
